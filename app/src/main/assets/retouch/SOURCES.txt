The edit mask is a Keeper heuristic based on lip and lower-jaw landmarks,
not a trained facial-hair segmentation model. Nose/lips are protected.
Face alignment, cheek colour matching and inward feathering run locally.
The app keeps original photos and saves edits as separate copies.
